Hello, I'm Shreyash.

I'm a software engineer at Goldman Sachs in Hyderabad, building backend systems for fund operations — AWS workflows that cut fund onboarding from days to under a minute, a recursive Java/SQL API for fee-rate discounts over nested fund hierarchies, and a reporting pipeline that took a 24-hour job down to seconds.

Before this I spent a couple of years on the research side of security. At CISPA I hunted for micro-architectural side-channels in the Linux kernel — reverse-engineering Intel's user-interrupt framework on Sapphire Rapids and building a timing channel that reads a neighbouring core's frequency from user space. At IIT Kanpur, with Prof. Angshuman Karmakar, I worked on cache attacks against post-quantum signatures, including a Prime+Probe on LESS that reconstructs the secret permutation. Along the way I also did automotive security at Jaguar Land Rover and C3iHub, and a summer of reinforcement learning for guidance at DCode Lab.

I did my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering at IIT Kanpur, with minors in Computer Systems, Machine Learning, and Philosophy. I like turning a fuzzy problem into a concrete experiment, building whatever infrastructure it needs, and writing down what I learn along the way.

Outside work I'm usually on a volleyball court, planning the next trip, or behind a camera. At IIT Kanpur I played on the volleyball team, led the Games & Sports Council, and founded the campus E-Sports club — so there's usually a game going somewhere.
